Age: 1,000+ years
Occupation: Mage
Frieren is a fantasy manga series written by Kanehito Yamada and illustrated by Tsukasa Abe. The story follows Frieren, an elf mage who was part of a hero's party that defeated the Demon King. After the battle, she embarks on a journey to understand human emotions and the fleeting nature of life, as she reflects on her experiences with her former companions.
